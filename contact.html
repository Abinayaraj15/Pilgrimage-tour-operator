Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for your divine yatra with Sacred Trails.

✦

What is the physical fitness level required for Kedarnath & Char Dham?

Kedarnath is situated at 3,583 meters altitude and requires either a helicopter flight (zero walking), a pre-booked palki/doli carried by four attendants, or an 18 km trek. For senior citizens, we strongly recommend our helicopter packages or verified palki seva. Our yatra doctors accompany every group with portable oxygen cylinders.

How do VIP Darshan passes work at Tirupati, Kashi, and Ayodhya?

We are registered with temple authorities and book official Seeghra Darshan / Sugam Darshan tokens well in advance. On the day of yatra, our resident coordinator escorts you through dedicated trust entry lines, significantly cutting down queue time from 6-8 hours to less than 45 minutes.

Can you cater to strict Jain (no root vegetables) and Ekadashi fasting meals?

Absolutely. All meals across our yatras are strictly 100% vegetarian. On prior notice, our accompanying maharajs prepare authentic Jain meals without potato, garlic, or onion, as well as special Phalahari meals (sabudana, fruits, rock salt) during Ekadashi and Navratri fasts.

What happens if mountain weather delays a flight or helicopter?

Safety in the Himalayas is paramount. In case of inclement weather, our flight operations team holds standby priority slots for the next immediate clear weather window. Comfortable resort stays, hot meals, and temple coordination are managed smoothly by our team without devotee distress.
