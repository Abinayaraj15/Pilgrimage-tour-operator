Sampurna Char Dham Maha Yatra (12 Days / 11 Nights)

Route: Haridwar • Barkot • Yamunotri • Uttarkashi • Gangotri • Guptkashi • Kedarnath • Badrinath • Rishikesh
1. Yamunotri Dham Surya Kund Thermal Spring
2. Gangotri Dham Bhagirathi Sacred River
3. Kedarnath Temple Highest Shiva Jyotirlinga (3,583 m)
4. Badrinath Dham Badri Vishal & Tapt Kund
Day 01

Arrival in Haridwar / Dehradun • Evening Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri

12:00 PM
Warm Welcome & Hotel Check-in

Reception at Dehradun Airport (DED) or Haridwar Railway Station with traditional garland welcome, briefing, and allocation of personalized yatra kits.

05:30 PM
Har Ki Pauri Maha Aarti VIP Enclosure

Reserved front-row VIP platform seating for the world-famous evening Ganga Aarti. Receive consecrated prasadam and floating diya offering.

08:00 PM
Pure Satvik Dinner & Yatra Orientation

Chef-curated pure Satvik dinner at hotel. Medical doctor conducts preliminary oxygen and blood pressure checks for high-altitude preparation.

Spiritual Significance: Haridwar literally means "Gateway to Lord Hari (Vishnu)" and "Gateway to Lord Hara (Shiva)". Bathing in the holy Ganga at Brahma Kund purifies the mind before ascending to the deities' Himalayan abodes.
Day 02

Haridwar to Barkot via Mussoorie (215 km / 7-8 hrs drive)

07:00 AM
Scenic Himalayan Ascent Begins

Board luxury air-conditioned tempo traveler. Travel through Mussoorie and picturesque mountain switchbacks along the holy Yamuna valley.

01:30 PM
Satvik Mountain Lunch Stop

Hot, freshly prepared Garhwali satvik lunch served in clean alpine rest house.

05:00 PM
Arrival at Barkot & Overnight Stay

Check into luxury mountain camp / deluxe resort overlooking Bandarpoonch peak. Evening satsang and rest for Yamunotri trek.

Day 03

Barkot to Yamunotri Dham & Return (Jankichatti 6 km Trek / Palki)

05:30 AM
Drive to Jankichatti & Ascend to Yamunotri

Board pre-booked Palkis, ponies, or commence guided trek up to the divine shrine of Goddess Yamuna (elevation 3,293 m).

10:30 AM
Surya Kund Cooking & Holy Darshan

Cook rice bundles in the boiling thermal waters of Surya Kund to take home as sacred prasad. VIP darshan of the black marble idol of Goddess Yamuna.

04:30 PM
Descent to Jankichatti & Return to Barkot

Comfortable descent and hot ginger tea at base. Rest and overnight stay in Barkot.

Spiritual Significance: Yamunotri is the source of the holy Yamuna River, daughter of the Sun God Surya and sister of Yama, the God of Death. A holy dip ensures freedom from the fear of untimely death.
Day 04

Barkot to Uttarkashi • Kashi Vishwanath Temple Darshan

Drive along Bhagirathi River to Uttarkashi (the Kashi of the North). Visit the ancient Kashi Vishwanath temple housing the massive divine Shakti spear (Trishul) which cannot be shaken by human hands. Overnight in riverfront resort.

Day 05

Uttarkashi to Gangotri Dham & Return (100 km / 4 hrs each way)

Drive through Harsil Valley (apple orchards and pine forests). Dip in the ice-cold Bhagirathi River, visit the 18th-century white granite temple of Maa Ganga, Bhagirath Shila where King Bhagirath meditated, and collect holy Gangajal.

Day 06-07

Guptkashi to Kedarnath Dham • Sunset Aarti & Overnight Stay at 3,583 m

Fly by helicopter or take premium Palki / pony from Gaurikund to Kedarnath base. Marvel at the 1,000-year-old stone temple built by the Pandavas surrounded by snow-covered peaks. Participate in the divine evening Shringar Aarti, sleep in heated VIP cottages, and attend 4:30 AM Maha Abhishek the next morning.

Day 08-09

Kedarnath to Badrinath Dham via Joshimath • Tapt Kund Snan

Scenic mountain descent to Pipalkoti and drive to Badrinath Dham along Alaknanda River. Holy bath in the natural sulphur hot spring of Tapt Kund, priority entry to Badri Vishal sanctum, visit Mana Village (India's first village, Vyas Gufa, and Saraswati River origin).

Day 10-12

Badrinath to Rishikesh • Prayag Confluences • Departure

Witness the five holy confluences (Panch Prayag: Vishnuprayag, Nandaprayag, Karnaprayag, Rudraprayag, and Devprayag). Arrive in Rishikesh, visit Laxman Jhula, Parmarth Niketan, and conclude the yatra with deep peace and lifelong memories.

Kashi, Prayagraj & Ayodhya Ram Mandir (6 Days / 5 Nights)

Varanasi • Sarnath • Prayagraj Sangam • Ayodhya Ram Janmabhoomi
1. Kashi Vishwanath & Ghats Evening Bajra Boat & Ganga Aarti
2. Prayagraj Triveni Sangam Ganga-Yamuna Holy Confluence

Day 01

Arrival in Varanasi • Ganga Aarti on Private Bajra Boat

Pickup from Varanasi Airport / Cantt Station. Check-in to Taj Ganges or Brijrama Palace. Evening private wooden boat ride along Manikarnika and Dashashwamedh Ghats with direct view of the grand Ganga Aarti.

Day 02

Kashi Vishwanath Corridor • Sankat Mochan • Annapurna

Sugam VIP Darshan at Kashi Vishwanath Golden Temple. Personalized Rudrabhishek with Vedic priest. Visit Annapurna Temple, Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple, and Durga Kund.

Day 03

Varanasi to Prayagraj • Triveni Sangam Snan • Bade Hanuman

Drive to Prayagraj (125 km / 2.5 hrs). Boat ride to the holy Triveni Sangam (confluence of Ganga, Yamuna, and mystical Saraswati) for holy dip and tarpan. Visit the rare reclining Bade Hanuman Temple and Anand Bhavan.

Day 04

Prayagraj to Ayodhya • Saryu Maha Aarti

Drive to the sacred city of Ayodhya. Check into luxury heritage stay. Evening peaceful visit to Ram Ki Paidi and attendance of the grand Saryu River Maha Aarti.

Day 05

Ram Janmabhoomi VIP Darshan • Hanuman Garhi • Kanak Bhavan

Guaranteed VIP pass for darshan of Ram Lalla at the grand newly consecrated Ram Mandir. Climb the holy steps of Hanuman Garhi, visit Kanak Bhavan, Dashrath Mahal, and Sita Ki Rasoi.

Day 06

Departure from Ayodhya / Lucknow Airport

Morning meditation by the Saryu. Chauffeur drop to Ayodhya Maharishi Valmiki Airport (AYJ) or Lucknow Airport (LKO) for onward return journey with lifelong memories.

Day 01-02

Madurai Meenakshi Amman Temple • Thirumalai Nayak Palace

Arrival in Madurai. Marvel at the four towering gopurams decorated with thousands of painted mythological sculptures. VIP entry into the sanctum sanctorum of Goddess Meenakshi and Lord Sundareswarar. Visit the 1,000-pillar hall and evening bedtime palliyarai procession.

Day 03-04

Rameswaram Jyotirlinga • 22 Theertham Sacred Snan

Cross the Pamban Sea Bridge. Early morning holy bath in the 22 consecrated theertham wells inside the temple complex with our dedicated purohit assistant. Darshan of the Spatika Lingam consecrated by Sri Rama. Visit Dhanushkodi and Ram Setu point.

Day 05-06

Kanyakumari • Bhagavathi Amman • Triveni Ocean Confluence

Drive to the southernmost tip of mainland India. Visit 3,000-year-old Kanyakumari Bhagavathi Amman Temple (diamond nose-ring that guides ships). Ferry to Vivekananda Rock Memorial and witness the sunset and moonrise over three oceans simultaneously.

Day 07-08

Thiruvananthapuram Padmanabhaswamy Sanctum • Departure

Traditional Kerala dhoti/mundu attire protocol for darshan of Lord Padmanabhaswamy reclining on Anantha Shesha through three sacred doors. Chauffeur drop to Trivandrum Airport (TRV).

12 Jyotirlinga Sacred Parikrama — Western & Central Shrines (7 Days / 6 Nights)

Mahakaleshwar • Omkareshwar • Somnath • Nageshwar (Dwarka) • Trimbakeshwar • Grishneshwar
Somnath & Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlingas Arabian Sea Aarti & Bhasma Aarti VIP Darshan

Day 01

Arrival in Indore • Drive to Ujjain • Shri Mahakal Lok Corridor

Arrive at Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport in Indore. Chauffeur-driven luxury transfer to the sacred city of Avantika (Ujjain) on the banks of holy Shipra River. Check into your heritage hotel. Evening private walking tour of the magnificent illuminated Shri Mahakal Lok corridor, witnessing 108 grand murals and sculptures celebrating Shiva Purana. Evening orientation and rest before pre-dawn aarti.

Day 02

Mahakaleshwar Bhasma Aarti VIP • Omkareshwar & Mamleshwar Island

At 3:30 AM, enter the sanctum sanctorum of Mahakaleshwar via our arranged VIP pass for the sacred Bhasma Aarti with fresh sacred ash offering. Post breakfast, scenic drive to Omkareshwar on Mandhata Island shaped like the sacred Om symbol in the Narmada River. Cross via suspension footbridge or private boat to perform jalabhishek at Omkareshwar and the adjacent ancient Mamleshwar Jyotirlinga. Witness the evening Narmada Deepdan aarti.

Day 03

Indore to Rajkot / Somnath • Prabhas Patan Sunset Seashore Aarti

Transfer for quick flight/express transit to Rajkot and drive across Saurashtra to Somnath (Prabhas Patan). Check into coastal hotel. As dusk sets over the Arabian Sea, witness the grand evening Aarti at the first among the twelve Jyotirlingas, where the sound of temple conches harmonizes with crashing ocean waves. Attend the awe-inspiring Light and Sound projection show depicting Somnath's immortal history.

Day 04

Somnath Rudrabhishek • Coastal Drive via Porbandar to Sacred Dwarka

Morning Laghu Rudrabhishek in the sanctum of Somnath with certified Vedic pandits. Begin scenic coastal highway drive towards Dwarka, pausing at Sudama Puri in Porbandar. Arrive in Dwarka by late afternoon. Attend the sacred Sandhya Aarti and ceremonial Dhwajarohan (flag-changing ritual) at the 2,500-year-old Dwarkadhish Jagat Mandir.

Day 05

Nageshwar Jyotirlinga Darshan • Bet Dwarka Island Ferry • Transit

Morning visit to Nageshwar Jyotirlinga (Darukavana), enshrining the mighty self-manifested lingam and towering 80-foot Shiva statue. Continue to Okha port for a scenic ferry ride across the sea to Bet Dwarka, the royal abode of Sri Krishna. Visit Rukmini Devi Temple and sacred Gopi Talav. Evening transfer to Rajkot/Ahmedabad for overnight luxury train or flight to Maharashtra.

Day 06

Arrival in Nashik • Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga & Kushavarta Kund

Arrive in Nashik and drive to the foothills of Brahmagiri Mountain at Trimbak. Purifying holy snan at Kushavarta Tirtha (the sacred origin pool of the Godavari River). Enter Trimbakeshwar Temple through VIP queue to receive darshan of the extraordinary three-faced Jyotirlinga representing Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva, topped with the jeweled Crown of the Pandavas. Evening Panchavati visit (Sita Gumpha and Kalaram Mandir).

Day 07

Grishneshwar Jyotirlinga Darshan • Ellora Kailasa Temple • Departure

Morning drive to Verul for darshan at Grishneshwar (Ghushmeshwar) Jyotirlinga, the sacred 12th and concluding shrine of the dwadasha jyotirlinga stotras. Marvel at the red basalt stone carvings and perform Mahapuja. Explore the neighboring UNESCO World Heritage Kailasa Temple (Cave 16), the world's greatest single-rock monolithic wonder. Chauffeur transfer to Aurangabad (IXU) or Pune (PNQ) airport for your return flight with divine memories and lifelong blessings.

High-Altitude Medical Protocol

Medical Science of Himalayan Acclimatization (Kedarnath, Badrinath, Yamunotri)

Ascending from the Indo-Gangetic plains (Haridwar at 314m) to Himalayan shrines like Kedarnath (3,583m) reduces atmospheric oxygen by approximately 35%. Our itineraries are medically structured to prevent Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS) through graded ascents, mandatory intermediate halts, and clinical monitoring.

The Graded Ascent Rule

We enforce mandatory overnight acclimatization at Guptkashi or Pipalkoti (1,319m) prior to entering high-altitude zones, allowing the body to synthesize red blood cells naturally.

4-Liter Hydration Mandate

Cold mountain air accelerates dehydration. Our vehicles provide warm water thermoses infused with cardamom and tulsi; yatris are guided to drink 3.5 to 4 liters daily to preserve circulation.

85% SpO2 Safety Baseline

Our tour managers record pulse oximeter readings every morning and evening. Any devotee recording below 85% is placed on immediate supplemental oxygen and examined by on-route physicians.

Smart Yatri Packing

What to Pack: North Himalayan vs. South Temple Circuits

Pack light, pack smart. Temple regulations and mountain microclimates require distinctly tailored apparel.

Himalayan & Cave Shrines

Char Dham, Kedarnath, Vaishno Devi, Amarnath
  • Thermal Underlayers: 2-3 pairs of moisture-wicking merino wool or fleece thermal inners.
  • Outer Weather Shell: Windproof down jacket rated to -5°C with waterproof hood.
  • Footwear: Broken-in trekking shoes with ankle support and deep tread rubber lugs + woolen socks.
  • Personal Health Kit: Prescribed medications, camphor inhalers, Diamox (if advised by doctor), and blister tape.
  • Gear: Telescopic trekking pole, waterproof poncho, 20L daypack, and 20,000mAh cold-resistant power bank.

South Dravidian & River Plains

Tirupati, Rameswaram, Madurai, Kashi, Puri
  • Mandatory Traditional Attire: White cotton/silk Dhotis with Angavastram for men; Sarees or Salwar Kameez with Dupatta for women.
  • Easy Slip-On Footwear: Slip-on sandals/chappals that can be swiftly stored at temple gopuram cloakrooms.
  • Thermal Stone Socks: Thick cotton socks to comfortably walk over sun-heated temple stone courtyards in afternoon hours.
  • Snan & Change Wear: Extra dry cotton towel and change set for the 22 sacred teertham snans at Rameswaram or Ganga snan.
  • Temple Puja Accessories: Small cloth tote for carrying offerings; strict prohibition of leather belts, wallets, and bags inside sanctums.
Devotional Conduct

Garbhagriha Etiquette & Temple Sanctity Norms

Sanatana temple traditions preserve immense spiritual energy through sacred customs. Devotees are requested to observe these sacred guidelines.

Clockwise Pradakshina

Always circumambulate deities in a clockwise direction. Never turn your back directly to the deity while exiting the inner sanctum.

Zero Leather Policy

Belts, wallets, watchstraps, and shoes made of animal hide are strictly barred. Our yatra sevaks provide safe lockers prior to sanctum entry.

Digital Silence

Photography inside Garbhagrihas is legally prohibited. Keep phones powered off to preserve the profound meditative vibrations of the shrine.

Prepared Sankalp Details

Keep your Gotra, Janma Nakshatra, Rashi, and names of family members noted in advance for the accompanying Vedic purohit during the holy sankalp.
